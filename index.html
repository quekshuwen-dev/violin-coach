🎻 How to tune your violin

1️⃣ Start with the A string (440 Hz)
2️⃣ Play the string slowly and watch the needle
3️⃣ Green + centred = perfectly in tune ✅
4️⃣ Then tune D → G → E
5️⃣ Always tune up to the note, never down
