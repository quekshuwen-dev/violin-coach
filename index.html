🎻 How to tune your violin

1️⃣ Start with the A string and match the centre
2️⃣ Bow slowly and evenly — a steady tone reads best
3️⃣ Green and within ±4¢ = in tune ✅
4️⃣ Then tune D → G → E
5️⃣ Always tune up to the note, never down
6️⃣ Pure fifths matches tuning by ear: E a touch sharp, D and G a touch flat
